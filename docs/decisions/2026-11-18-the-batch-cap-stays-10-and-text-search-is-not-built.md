# 2026-11-18 - The batch cap stays 10, and text search is not built

- Task: `api-discover` (owner decision 2026-11-18; recorded by the implementer, 2026-11-18).
- Context: the production reading of `GET /api/v1/records-by-id` with 20 ids in 20 chunks at `f8bb4808` was 41.5 ms cold and 24.7 ms burst p50 (Pages Metrics), about 2 ms an id. The cap fell from 20 to 10 on 2026-11-18 as a one-time exception to "a cap may rise, never fall" (decision "A batch read has its own path, and search rows carry a built summary"). The reading at 10 ids after the deploy of `06847187` is 7.9 ms cold and 15.6 ms burst p50 (an upper bound: 2 foreign requests in the minute). The last 12 hours showed 0 errors and 0 Exceeded CPU in 406 requests, including one spike of 143 requests. Production cold search already costs 11 to 22 ms (a two-word search 11.1 ms with `match=word`, `limit=50` 19.7 ms and 22.5 ms with `fields=summary`), and Node readings run 2 to 14 times below production on these paths.
- Decision (owner, 2026-11-18): the batch cap stays at 10 and is not raised. The reading at 10 ids is under 10 ms cold, but the burst median is above 10 ms, so a higher cap has no proof of safety.
- Decision (owner, 2026-11-18): search over descriptions (`in: "text"`) is not built. A scan of about 400 KB of description text has no headroom in a search path that already reads 11 to 22 ms cold. The need is covered by `match=word`, the opt-in `fields=summary` and `get_records`.
- Rejected: raise the cap back to 20 (the reading at 20 ids is 4 times the free-plan limit cold and 2.5 times at burst p50); build `in: "text"` behind a Node benchmark (the benchmark measures Node, and production runs 2 to 14 times slower on these paths, so it cannot show the production cost); a paid Worker plan now (the trigger stays any Exceeded CPU or 1102, and none was counted); a statline file per table part (only if the production reading of `eq_weapon` part 3, 12.7 ms cold and not fully cold, is confirmed worse by a repeat).
- Consequences: a client that needs more than 10 records makes more than one `get_records` call. The "expected to return to 20" sentence of the batch decision and of `CONTRACTS.md` section 6 no longer applies. A text search would need a new decision with a production reading and a CPU plan. Cold readings above 10 ms stay inside the envelope the owner accepted on 2026-11-13; the trigger for a paid plan is unchanged.
- Status: accepted.
- Evidence: `.claude/README.md`, "Quotas, recovery and failure" (the readings of 2026-11-18); `docs/specs/CONTRACTS.md` section 6, "Batch read" and "Search".
