# 2026-10-21 - Each Save to my items press makes a new copy, also of a catalog record

- Superseded in part by "A Save to my items copy leaves out its original's set" (2026-10-22): a copy keeps or copies no set.
- Task: `catalog-copy` (owner, 2026-10-20: a catalog copy is «Сохранить себе» on the catalog record, consistent with copying another account's item, into «Хоумбрю» with no section, no stored link, the catalog picture, set and rule cards named, list rows kept; owner, 2026-10-21, decision 42: a copy per press on both sources, numbered names).
- Decision: «Сохранить себе» on another account's item and on a catalog record (`#/i/<id>`, its record modal) writes one more own item at each press through one path (`AppState.saveItem`, `lib/homebrew.ts` `copyRows`), under a new key from `newIds()`; the set and rule cards it copies keep `cardKeyFor` (derived), so a second press names them. The button stays «Сохранить себе»; «Сохранено» is gone. The name is the source's, else the smallest free «(N)» from 2 per language (`copyName`, cut to 120 code points). A catalog record copies through `copyContentOf`: kind `equip` with a stat block, catalog `set`, `refs`, `craft`, `craft_from` and `eq.line` kept, table and card-set placement left out. A press whose answer was lost and whose copy the read does not find keeps its key in memory under `<userId>\n<ref>`; the next press of that source sends it again, and `import_homebrew` skips a held key; a limit or a refusal drops it. Behaviour: `docs/specs/FEATURES.md`, "Records".
- Rejected: one copy per source under the derived key with «Сохранено» (the owner wants a copy per press); a filled editor at `#/homebrew/new` (not consistent with copying another account's item); a stored link to the catalog record; own copies of catalog cards; a relink of a catalog record's list rows; parsing a «(N)» in the source's name (a source named «Меч (2)» becomes «Меч (2) (2)»).
- Accepted trade-off: two tabs that press at once can give two copies one name, and when both relink the same rows the rows end on the copy whose relink ran last, the other copy unlinked; a tab closed before the relink leaves an unlinked copy; a reload or a closed tab after a lost answer whose copy landed loses the kept key, so the next press makes «X (2)» and «X» stays unlinked. Nothing stored is lost.
- Supersedes in part "A key the account does not hold becomes a derived key" (2026-10-10): a «Сохранить себе» item takes a new key per press; its cards keep derived keys.
