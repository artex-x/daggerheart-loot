# 2026-10-28 - Addresses are paths, with the language prefix and `?v` in the bar

- Task: `path-urls` (owner, 2026-10-28, Q1-Q6 each option A).
- Decision: every route is a path (Q1); an English address starts with `/en/`, a record keeps `/i/en/<id>`, a stored language wins and the bar follows the screen (Q2); the bar carries `?v` on a record, `/h/` and `/s/` (Q3); URLs are root-absolute, `base: '/'` (Q4); an old `#/` address opens and becomes its path in one step (Q5); the record modal leaves the bar (Q6). Homes: `docs/specs/ROUTES.md` "Path form", `CONTRACTS.md` sections 1 and 5, `I18N.md`, `META.md` sections 4 and 8, `FEATURES.md` "Consistency rules", rule 17.
- Rejected: Q1 B, paths for the card families only (two address forms in the bar); Q2 B, `/en/i/<id>` with `/i/en/<id>` as an alias (a second family of 1459 files or a rewrite); Q2 C, no language in the address (an English bar hands out the Russian card); Q3 B, `?v` only on the copy buttons (a bar-copied card is cached for good); Q4 B, relative strings and `<base href="/">` (risk to the print page's inline SVG `url(#...)` and the skip link); Q4 C, relative paths at every depth (every path through the worker, so a quota burn reaches the whole site); Q5 B, drop the `#/` addresses (every link and bookmark since the site began lands home); Q6 B, the modal writes the record's path (an overlay state, left to a later task if asked).
- Accepted trade-off: the app runs only at a host root; the router keeps two address forms for good; a bar copied while a modal is open hands out the page under it.
- Card paths answer the app document: `/i/<id>`, `/i/en/<id>` and `/en/` are static app documents with the stub's card fields in the head and, for a record, its readable text in a `<noscript>` block (owner, 2026-10-28, keeping "AI assistants read a compact index and the record pages") (`tools/app-documents.js`), and the worker answers `/h/`, `/s/`, `/p/` and `/tables/` with the app document it reads through `env.ASSETS`, the card in its head; a failed read answers the card page. Homes: `CONTRACTS.md` sections 4 and 5, `.claude/README.md` "Preview host".
- Rejected for the card paths: a user-agent split (an unknown crawler gets the redirect); the redirect page kept (a pasted bar address and a link differ by one hop, and the page flashes); a worker for every route (a quota burn reaches the whole site); `_redirects` proxying to the worker (the same quota cost, and unverified on Pages); an English shell read (the shells differ only in the card block and `<html lang>`).
- Accepted trade-off for the card paths: `no-store` on a worker answer keeps it out of the back-forward cache; each record document grows by its text.
- Supersedes in part "The site moves to Cloudflare Pages and the old host mirrors its files" (2026-10-28): a card path answers the app document, not a page that redirects to the app.
