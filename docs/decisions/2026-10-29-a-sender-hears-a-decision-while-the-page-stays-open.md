# 2026-10-29 - A sender hears a decision while the page stays open

- Task: `request-notices` (owner, 2026-10-28, from user feedback; Q1 = B).
- Decision: when the owner applies or declines a request, `purchase_requests_broadcast()` also sends `request` with `{ request, status, partial }` to the request's own share topic while that share is active (`20261029120000_request_decision_share.sql`, its send in its own nested `exception when others` block, so it never fails the decision or the owner message). The sending tab keeps the ids it sent with success on this page load (memory only, the newest 20) and toasts the decision of its own requests; the bar's line under the actions says what was sent and how long it waits, then the decision. Homes: `docs/specs/FEATURES.md`, "Purchase requests: the send"; `STATE.md`, "A purchase request leaves nothing in the browser".
