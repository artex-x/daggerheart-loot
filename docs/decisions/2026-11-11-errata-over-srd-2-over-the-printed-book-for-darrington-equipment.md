# 2026-11-11 - Errata over SRD 2.0 over the printed book for Darrington Press equipment

- Task: `errata-audit` (planner, 2026-11-11; the trigger was a report that the Revolver `f37` prints d6+1).
- Decision: a Core, Hope & Fear or campaign-frame record carries the official errata first
  (`Daggerheart-Errata-September9th2025.pdf`, the Hope & Fear errata of 2026-08-25), then SRD 2.0
  (`DH_SRD_2_2026_08_25.pdf`), then the printed book. Each place where a record departs from its
  printed source is a row in a `docs/provenance/` register.
- Rejected: the printed Core book (the owner's copy is a pre-errata printing: Spear d10+2 with
  Cumbersome, Revolver d6+1, Sledgehammer's Massive on Agility); SRD 2.0 over the errata (its
  Buckler reads "your available Armor Score", half of the errata line "your available Armor
  Slots", and its text layer has none of the nine Tier 3 Core magic weapons from Axe of Fortunis
  to Ilmari's Rifle).
- Evidence: on 2026-11-11 the 381 Core and Hope & Fear records and the 92 frame records were
  compared with SRD 2.0 and the errata; the only departures were the Revolver line `f37`-`f40`
  (d6, errata p. 317: d8) and Sledgehammer `f6` (Agility, errata p. 275: Evasion).
