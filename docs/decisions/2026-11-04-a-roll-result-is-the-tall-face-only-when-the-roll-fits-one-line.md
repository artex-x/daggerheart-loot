# 2026-11-04 - A roll result is the tall face only when the roll fits one line

- Amends "The roll pages share one layout, and a result is a card face with its own row" (2026-11-04): a roll that does not fit one line draws row faces.
- Task: `57` (owner, 2026-11-04, chat: "also for classic and alternative rolls page look good only when 2 items are visible, for 4 or 1 it doesn't fit to the screen fully, can we show more compact view?").
- Decision: a result is the tall face (the picture 4:3, the number over it) only inside a `faces` container of at least 641 px, which `OrGrid` names for one or two results and `RollPanel`'s results always; a tall face is at most 380 px wide. Otherwise it is the row face of the phone, 96 px beside the text with no number. Measured in the built app (RU): at 1180x720 four results on `/roll/std` end their names at 562 px and the roll at 747 (was 1049 and 1196); one result is 380 px wide and ends at 665 (was 791 px wide, 973); at 1919x980 every count ends inside the window (four at 747, the alternate crit at 820). Specs: `FEATURES.md` "Rolling"; `DESIGN.md` "The Roll Page Rule", "Cards", "Badges".
- Rejected: option A, the picture cropped by the count (a 2:1 strip of square art, and row 2 still below the fold at 1180x720); option C, a «Компактно» switch per device (a control and a stored key the owner did not ask for, and the default still fails the owner's case).
- Accepted trade-off: a four-result roll and every roll in a results column under 641 px (761 to 1014 px, 601 to about 688 px) lose the large picture and the card's number; at 1180x720 the last action row of a four-result roll ends below the fold, and on an alternate crit the last name too (744 px in RU).
