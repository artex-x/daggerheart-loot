# 2026-10-07 - A list's change log shares the requests' view and the database's clean-up

- Task: `persist-7h-homebrew-page` (owner, 2026-10-03, W1-c and W1-e; retention and Q2 = B, owner, 2026-10-06).
- Decision: a change of a linked item by its author writes one row per list and item into `list_notices` (`changed`, or `deleted` with the item's names), only for lists of other owners, from the item, source and card triggers; the list's owner reads and deletes them, nobody inserts or updates them. One `notice` message per list owner per transaction goes on the owner topic. `mark_list_read(uuid)` sets `read_at = now()` on the list's unread notices and requests when a visible page draws them. A notice expires 1 hour after it is read, or 30 days after its last change while unread. A purchase request takes the same rule: it expires 1 hour after its first read, apply or decline, or 30 days after it was sent while unread; «Принять» and «Отклонить» work until then. `lifecycle_cleanup()` deletes both in the hour after they expire (`docs/specs/META.md` section 3).
- Rejected: one table for requests and notices (requests are written by `anon` through a bounded function, with lines and a status; notices by triggers, with neither); a decision window of 1 hour from creation with an expired row left visible (the planner's recommendation, which the owner declined); a client-chosen read time (a row could outlive its retention).
- Accepted trade-off: a GM may apply a request days after the player sent it, against the stock of that day; an unread request counts against the 10 pending of its list for up to 30 days.
- Amends "Purchase requests are written only by a bounded function any link holder calls" (2026-09-26): a request expires 1 hour after it is read or 30 days after it is sent, not 1 hour after it is sent.
- Amends "Lifecycle data is deleted by the database on a schedule" (2026-10-07): the clean-up deletes requests and notices once they expire.
