# 2026-10-29 - A new purchase request toasts on every page

- Task: `request-notices` (owner, 2026-10-28, from user feedback; Q2 = B).
- Decision: an owner sees a new purchase request on every page as the lowest-priority action toast, «Новый запрос по списку «%s»», with «Открыть» / «Показать», which opens the list and focuses its requests panel; it waits behind any other toast, a plain one too, and gives way to any later toast. The tab title counts the unread requests that have not expired, «(2) Мои списки - ...». Client-only: the owner topic's `request` message and the heads read already carry it. Homes: `docs/specs/FEATURES.md`, "Purchase requests: the owner" and "Chrome"; `STATE.md`, "Session".
- Rejected: a dot on the account control and a count pill on «Мои списки» in the account menu (owner, 2026-10-28, Q2 = B: the toast and the title count are enough, and rule 4 keeps the menu's words); a sound on an arrival (browsers block audio before a gesture, and it disturbs the game at the table); a vibration (absent on iOS Safari, and an unasked buzz at the table); a toast for a change-log notice (an author's edit asks nothing of the owner and does not expire an hour after a read); system notifications and the installed app's badge (deferred: a permission prompt, and a push to a closed app needs a push server).
- Rejected for narrow screens: a shorter toast (fails WCAG 2.2.1 for a person who cannot reach a toast that never takes the focus) and a smaller or top toast (a second toast look); at 360 px the toast keeps the look, the place and the 7000 ms of every action toast (owner away; the default was taken, 2026-10-29).
- Accepted trade-off: another tab or device keeps its title count until its next requests read, at most 5 minutes with a live feed, because a read mark sends no message; a later toast drops the arrival toast, and the title count still carries it.
