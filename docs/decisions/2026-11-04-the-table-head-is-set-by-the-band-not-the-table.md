# 2026-11-04 - The table head is set by the band, not the table

- Task: `57` (owner, 2026-11-04, chat: "I also don't like much that some divs are jumping during navigation, is there a better way?").
- Decision: on `/tables/*` the head's geometry follows the viewport band only. Measured causes: the navigation was one flex item of the wrapping title row, so it sat beside the title or under it by the width of its widest row, the seven supplement tabs (1180 px: the search row 142 to 238.5 px between tables); the tab row was absent on `homebrew` and two lines on the supplements from 761 to 999 px; a two-line picker added a line's descent up to 760 px (2 px). Now from 1000 px the chips sit beside the title and wrap inside their own box and the tabs take the next row; from 761 to 999 px both levels take their own rows; the tab row holds every group's tabs unseen, so it keeps the tallest one's height on every table; the picker row is a flex box. Specs: `FEATURES.md` "Tables and search"; `DESIGN.md` "Layout", "The Head Order Rule" (it replaced "A steady head" on 2026-11-05).
- Rejected: option B, the per-table head with space reserved for late content only (the chips still sit beside the title on one table and under it on another, the jump the owner filmed); a per-table `min-height` patch with no structure (it hides the symptom on one table); the chips under the title from 1000 px (the first row at 1180x720 at 290.5 px, past the 288 px first-screen limit); empty toolbar slots for the controls a table lacks (a hidden control stays a focus stop or an `aria-hidden` focusable).
- Accepted trade-off: from 761 to 999 px every table reserves the supplements' two tab lines (the first row 43 px lower than before on most tables); from 1000 to about 1140 px in Russian the chips take two lines beside the title; the toolbar's view switch still moves sideways where a table adds «Фильтры», «Свои предметы» or «Ссылка на таблицу», and the filter column draws on a table with filters only.
- Remediation (batch 7b review, 2026-11-04): from 1000 to about 1139 px in Russian the chips and the reserved tab row each take two lines, so the head is about 173 px (chips 89, tabs 179.5 at 1050; reviewer's probe) and the rows start at about 330.5 px at 1050x720, past the 288 px first-screen limit; before this batch they started at 240.5-290.5 px. This is a number that does not meet the goal but is measured, and `tests/app/sweep.js` caps it at 180 px at 1050. The homebrew group's source tabs are left out of the reserved tab row: a GM with 12 sources of 80 characters would make it 3-6 lines on every table and grow it after the own items load. The trade-off: `/tables/homebrew` with many sources draws its own taller row, a jump on that one table.
