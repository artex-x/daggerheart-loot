Privacy

This page says what the Daggerheart Loot Generator stores, where it is kept, and how to delete it. The site is a personal project run by artex-x.

What the site stores now

  • If you do not sign in, nothing about you is stored on a server. The site has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies.
  • Your lists, the language and your other settings stay in your browser's local storage, on your device. To remove them, clear the site data in the browser.
  • A list link carries the list after the # sign. The browser never sends that part to a server, so only the people who have the link can read the list.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and receives the usual technical data of each request, such as your IP address. The GitHub privacy statement covers that data.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Google or Discord. The site then stores:

  • your lists, your homebrew items, their pictures and your settings;
  • the Google or Discord identity that the provider sends at sign-in, and your email address. Supabase Auth keeps them; the site shows your email to you only.

Supabase keeps this data in its EU West (Ireland) region. Nothing is sold or given to anyone else.

Share links

A share link to a list is a player link or a GM link. Anybody who holds a link can read the player view or the GM view of that list until you replace the link with a new one or revoke it. The player view leaves out the GM notes. The link itself is kept with the list, and only you can read it there, so you can copy it again at any time. A share link to a homebrew item works the same way. A share link never shows your name or your email.

Deleting your data

If you sign in, you delete your account on the account page. The deletion is immediate: your lists, homebrew items, pictures, settings and share links leave the live database at once, and every share link stops working. A backup made before the deletion can keep the data until that backup is replaced.

If a Google or Discord account is already in use

One Google or Discord account can belong to one account here only. If you connect one that another of your accounts already uses, the site refuses. To join the two, sign in to the account you want to drop, export its data and delete it. Then sign in to the account you keep, import the data and connect the Google or Discord account again.

Contact

For a privacy question, a deletion request or a report of an abused share link, write to daggerheart.loot@gmail.com.
