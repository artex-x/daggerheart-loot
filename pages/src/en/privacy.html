Privacy

This page says what the Daggerheart Loot Generator stores, where it is kept, and how to delete it. The site is a personal project run by artex-x.

What the site stores now

  • If you do not sign in, nothing about you is stored on a server except requests to a list's owner (below), and they are linked neither to you nor to your device. The site has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies.
  • Your lists, the language and your other settings stay in your browser's local storage, on your device. To remove them, clear the site data in the browser.
  • A list link carries the list after the # sign. The browser never sends that part to a server, so only the people who have the link can read the list.
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts the site at daggerheart-loot.pages.dev and receives the usual technical data of each request, such as your IP address. The Cloudflare privacy policy covers that data. GitHub Pages serves the old address, artex-x.github.io/daggerheart-loot: it serves the same files and sends you on to the new address, and GitHub receives the same technical data, which the GitHub privacy statement covers.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Google or Discord. The site then stores:

  • your lists, your homebrew items with their sources, sections and pictures, your set cards and rule cards, and your settings;
  • the Google or Discord identity that the provider sends at sign-in, and your email address. Supabase Auth keeps them; the site shows your email to you only.

Supabase keeps this data in its EU West (Ireland) region. Nothing is sold or given to anyone else.

Share links

A share link to a list is a player link or a GM link. Anybody who holds a link can read the player view or the GM view of that list until you replace the link with a new one or revoke it. The player view leaves out the GM notes and the entries marked GM only; such an item still opens at its own address. The link itself is kept with the list, and only you can read it there, so you can copy it again at any time. A share link shows the homebrew items in the list as they are now.

Pictures

A picture you add to a homebrew item is kept as a file in Supabase Storage under a random name. Its address is public: anybody who opens the item, or a list that holds it, can open the picture. Two of your items that show the same picture share its address, so a reader can tell they are from one account. When no item of yours names a picture any more - you replaced or removed it, deleted the item or deleted your account - the database deletes the file, usually within a minute, and within a few hours if a request fails; the address can still answer for up to a day after that, because a cached copy stays valid for that long. A player who saves your item to their own items gets their own copy of the picture; deleting yours does not delete theirs. The backup keeps pictures encrypted for at most 30 days. The data archive holds no pictures.

Homebrew item addresses

Each homebrew item has its own address. Anybody who has the address can open the item, signed out too, with the items it relates to: the items it is made from or upgrades to, and the other items of its set. Nobody can list your items without their addresses. The item's address also shows when it was last changed. Neither a share link nor an item's address shows your name or your email. If another user adds your item to their list, their list shows the item as it is now: they see your changes, and when you delete the item, its row leaves their list, which keeps only the item's name and the fact that it was deleted, for at most 30 days. If another user saves a copy of your item to their own items, the copy is theirs: it does not change with your item and stays when you delete the item or your account.

Print sets

Printing cards needs an account. Each print keeps a set in your account: the card numbers and their counts, without notes or prices. Any signed-in person who has its /#/p/ address or its print set link (daggerheart-loot.pages.dev/p/...) can open it. A set is deleted 90 days after it was last opened, and with the account. The "Download my data" archive does not hold them.

Requests to a list's owner

Anybody who holds a link to a list can send its owner a request for the items they picked. A request keeps the items, their counts, the kind of link (the player link or the GM link) and the time it was sent; it holds no name, email, account or IP address of the sender. Supabase, like Cloudflare, receives the usual technical data of each request to its server. A request expires 30 days after it was sent, or an hour after the owner first sees it, whichever comes first. The site deletes it within the hour after it expires; with the owner's list or account, at once. The sender's browser keeps nothing about the request.

Deleting your data

If you sign in, you delete your account on the account page. The deletion is immediate: your lists, homebrew items, set and rule cards, print sets, settings and share links leave the live database at once, and every share link stops working. A backup made before the deletion is kept encrypted for at most 30 days; after that the deleted data is gone from it too. Your items also leave other users' lists at once: their rows go, and those lists keep only each item's name and the fact that it was deleted, for at most 30 days. Copies of your items that other users saved to their own items stay with them: they do not change, and they are not deleted with your account.

If a Google or Discord account is already in use

One Google or Discord account can belong to one account here only. If you connect one that another of your accounts already uses, the site refuses. To join the two, sign in to the account you want to drop, export its data (the "Download my data" archive) and delete it. Then sign in to the account you keep, load the archive on the My items page first, then on the My lists page, and connect the Google or Discord account again.

Link previews

The links the site copies for a homebrew item, a list (share links), a print set and a table go to daggerheart-loot.pages.dev (Cloudflare). When such a link is pasted into a messenger, the messenger fetches that address and shows a card: for an item, its name, description, picture and crafting and set lines; for a list, its name, the number of entries and the start of the note for players (a GM note never appears on a card); for a print set, only a generic text, nothing of the set; for a table, its name and the number of entries. A person who opens the link gets the same card and goes on to the site's page at once. Cloudflare receives the usual technical data of each request: an IP address and the link's address with the item id, the key of the share link, the print set id or the filter segment (a filter can hold the key of your own source); the Cloudflare privacy policy covers that data. The card server stores nothing. A messenger can keep its copy of a card after the item or the list is changed, the item is deleted or the share link is revoked. A print set link can open in the browser rather than in the installed app; you may need to sign in there.
