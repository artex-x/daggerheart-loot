Install as an app

You can put this site on your phone or computer as an app: its own icon, its own window, no address bar. Nothing is downloaded from a store - it is the same site.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) next to the address bar.
  3. Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

Chrome may also offer the install in a bar at the bottom of the screen - that is the same path.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iOS versions, this button can be in the menu with three dots.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add".

Lists in the installed app on an iPhone are stored apart from Safari's. The lists in your account open everywhere: sign in both in Safari and in the app.

Desktop (Chrome, Edge)

Press the install icon in the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose the item that starts with "Install". It can be in a submenu.

Connection

The installed app needs a connection, the same as the site. It does not open offline.
