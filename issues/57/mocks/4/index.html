Changes from direction R and from plan.md

  • The item page at 360 is a page: the art on top, the actions after the text, the primary on its own line. R drew the item page at 360 as the dialog's sheet over the tables.
  • The sheet's fixed bar holds «В список» on its own line and «Поделиться» with «Ещё» under it, so no label is cut at 360.
  • Signed out, the menu item reads «Войти и сохранить копию в «Мои предметы»» and its note goes (C19, the clarity inventory); /h/ shows the same words on its button in place of the «Войдите, и копия...» box.
  • The error page shows the path form of the address (/i/nope, /en/i/nope).
  • The catalog behind the dialog at 1180 shows the batch 3 first-screen layout.
  • Review mocks-1: M4-1 - «Ещё» opens as a bottom sheet at 360. M4-2 - one label, «Сохранить себе» / "Save to My items", on every page and in every state (it replaces «Копия в мои предметы» and the signed-out «Войти и сохранить копию...»); «Мои предметы» is capitalised; the relink line is the item's second line when a list of the reader links the item, and the plain note «Копия попадёт в «Хоумбрю» и больше не будет меняться...» goes (owner). M4-3 - the states /h/ loading, /h/ failed read, /i/<key> failed read and the own item with «Изменить» are drawn. Spec change to name in FEATURES.md "Records" (batch 4): the not-found title «Предмет не найден» becomes «Предмета по этому адресу нет» / "No item at this address", which says the cause. M4-4 - the item page, the dialog and the roll cards crop the square art to 4:3 on screen; batch 4 names that crop window in DESIGN.md "Shapes".
