List head pattern (final, 2026-10-29)

One head for every list page: the catalog (/tables/*), the Items tab of /homebrew, /lists, /lists/<uuid> and /s/<token>. Mock 3 owns it; mocks 6, 8 and 9 take it. A page that departs names the departure here and in FEATURES.md.

Part Rule RU / EN
Search The first control of the search row on every list page. At 360 it is at least 140 px wide. placeholder «Поиск» / "Search"
Filters After the search at 360 on a page with a filter panel; at 1180 the filter column takes its place. Plain at N = 0; lit while N > 0 (the toggle: a gold label on a 45% gold border, no fill). «Фильтры» / "Filters", «Фильтры · 2» / "Filters · 2"
Select The last control of the search row at every width. No icon: a check with a label is the neutral pressed state, so a check would read as "on". Hidden while the selection mode is on. «Выбрать» / "Select"
View switch Its own control, never inside «Ещё» (C57): beside the page title at 360, in the search row at 1180, on a page that has a view. «Список» / «Сетка», "List" / "Grid"
More On the title row at 360; it holds the page's rare actions (the table link, the pin, the data panel). At 1180 they show in the head and the search row. The pin is always «Открывать при запуске» / «Открывается при запуске». «Ещё» / "More"
Count Once, and only while the view is narrowed: in the section head at 360, in the search row at 1180 (as search says nothing under its cap). «Показано 23 из 458» / "23 of 458 shown"
Filter-active state One state on every list page: «Фильтры · N» lit and the count «Показано 23 из 458». The reset and the filter link (rule 17) sit where the filters are set: the filter column head at 1180, the filter sheet head at 360 (the sheet foot keeps «Сбросить»). No pill row at 360 (as on /homebrew): with it the first row of a filtered table started at 325 px at 360, past the 256 px limit. «Сбросить» / "Reset", «Ссылка на фильтры» / "Link to these filters"
Selection bar One bar, sticky at the bottom of the list, on every list page: the count, the page's batch actions, «Снять выделение» last. The list keeps a bottom padding equal to the bar height, so the bar never covers its last row. A ticked row shows a lit ground and the check, with no side stripe. «Выбрано: 1» / "1 selected", «Снять выделение» / "Clear the selection"

Spec change to name in FEATURES.md "Tables and search" (batch 3): no pill row at 360 on any list page; at 1180 the filter column shows the picks. A table in another group takes three taps at 360 (the picker, the group, the table) against two today: the trade-off of C7, which puts the two chip levels into one labelled sheet.
