Changes from direction R and from plan.md

  • One layout for the nine roll modes (M5-5). At 360: the H1 is visually hidden (the lit mode control names the page); the mode's roll controls (the dice, or a picker beside the roll button); the results line «Бросок N» with «Настроить ▾» at its end (M5-1), which opens the settings above the results; compact result cards (a 96 px art beside the text) under 600 px, the full card face from 600 px (M5-7, owner). At 1180: two panes, every control in the left pane, the pin and the help by the H1.
  • The settings panel holds the manual entry, Источник and Тип (std), the Hope and Fear fields (alt), «Скопировать все четыре», the pin and the help button (M5-2). «Настроить» takes a settings icon, not the filter funnel (M5-9).
  • The rarity moves onto each result card as a badge on /roll/std; the result heading is «Бросок N» on every roll page, with no instruction in it (M5-8).
  • The compact card hides the roll-number badge at 360: the results line names the roll.
  • The mode row of mock 2 at 360 takes 6 px above and below with 40 px chips; the dice and the chips stay above the 24 px target size of WCAG 2.5.8.
