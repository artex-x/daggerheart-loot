Feedback round: F1 and F2

This page draws the answer to two pieces of end-user feedback (owner, 2026-11-03). The reasons, the costs and the owner questions are in issues/57/feedback.md. The frames are first screens at a phone size, not tall pages, so the rows per screen are visible. Records and pictures are real. Impeccable critique has not run: the orchestrator runs it on this folder.

Recommended set

  • A, the title and its buttons: batch 5 is the answer. No roll page draws the buttons beside a title up to 760 px. No extra rule.
  • B, the header: the brand row scrolls away and the three jobs stay sticky, up to 760 px. CSS only. No hide-and-show logic.
  • C, the rows: C1b, one compact row, 77 px, with one clamped line of effect or stat, on /lists/<uuid> and /s/ up to 760 px. A press unfolds the full row. Nothing is stored. C1 is the same row with no text line.
  • D, search and filter: the search row from 8 entries on the owner's page, the filter sheet at every width, the filter in memory.

C. The rows (F2-2)

Revised after the critique. The owner's row has no sign. The body is the button and a 20 px chevron ends it. The quantity is a 42x44 px field apart at the far right, so two targets read as two. The row stays 57 px, and C1b is 77 px.

C1 or C1b

C1b adds one line for the comparison a player makes. A weapon leads with damage and range. In Russian at 360 px one line holds 5% of the record texts whole, so a long item text still needs a press. Recommended: C1b on both pages, because players choose by effect and it still shows four times today's rows. The trade-off: 2.6 fewer rows than C1 at 360x640 with B1.
