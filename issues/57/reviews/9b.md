## Blockers

B1. **At 360 px the notes of an open compact row draw in an 88 px column and break inside
words.** Shots `list-note__360__ru.png` and `list-notes__360__ru.png`: «ДЛЯ ИГРОКОВ» reads
«ИГР/ОКО/В», «Светится» reads «Свет/ится», «Проклят» reads «Прок/лят». Cause: `ListPage.svelte`
`.rnote { flex: 0 0 100%; padding: 0 11px 11px 146px; }` applies to the compact row too. 9a
had `.lrow.compact .rnote { margin: 4px 0 0 }`, and 9b deleted it. The open note area in a
compact row gets the same 146 px indent, so the field is about 170 px wide at 360. This is the
phone-first player and owner surface (DESIGN.md "The Phone-First Rule"). It breaks WCAG 1.4.10
reflow in spirit (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/reflow.html) and the Whole Message
Rule (a label cut inside a word; https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/overflow-content/).
No check caught it: the goldens are accessibility trees, and the `typo.js` Whole Message step
reads 1180 only. Fix: `.lrow.compact .rnote { padding: 0; margin: 4px 0 0; }`. Then re-take
the two shots at 360 RU and EN with a players' note and a GM note filled, and with the area open.
Add a geometric guard to case 105 or 107 at 360: the `.rnote` box is at least the row body's
width less 24 px.

B2. **The requests panel breaks The Seal Rule and mock 9.** `RequestsPanel.svelte` `.reqpanel`
keeps a 45% gold border and a 6% gold ground. `.total`, `.st-ok` and `.nnew` are
`--gold-soft`, and each shown request draws its own gold «Принять» (two gold fills in one
panel in `list-requests-open__1180__ru.png`). DESIGN.md "The Seal Rule" says: "A notice that
waits, the list card's «Новое в списке (N)», is a neutral chip: nobody chose it". It also sets
the budget at "one primary action per panel". Mock 9 (`list-requests-open__1180__ru.png`) draws
a plain plate, a Chalk bold total and the gold «Принять» on the first request only. 9b's
objective is "the share, roll and requests panels take mock 9's form", so this is in scope, not
a later question. Fix (CSS and one `variant` expression, no golden moves): the plate is the
`Panel` ground (`--line` border, the plate gradient). `.total` is `--txt` with its `b` at 650,
and `.st-ok` and `.nnew` are `--muted2`. The apply button of `shown[0]` is `primary`, and the
apply buttons of the other requests are plain. Remove the handoff Deferred line "the requests
panel keeps its gold frame".

B3. **The 324 px exception at 360 is not acceptable as built. Compact the folded strip, then
measure again.** The built folded strip is about 56 px tall with an 18 px bottom margin
(`padding: 12px 14px`, an `h2` at `--t-body`, a 32 px button). The strip in mock 9 is about 36
px. Up to 760 px, when the panel is folded, take `padding: 6px 12px`, `margin-bottom: 10px` and
a strip height of about 40 px. This saves about 24-28 px, so the first entry is near the owner's

Alternative (not recommended for this pass): put «Новое в списке (2) ▾» on the meta line, as 9a
did with «Заметки ▾». This removes the exception (about 250 px). The cost is a weaker

`faces` container picks its row face. The description then runs about 18 characters a line for
8 lines (`list-roll__1180__ru.png`), and mock 9 draws the 4:3 card face. The handoff does not

make the face column wide enough for `RollResult`'s face threshold, or pass the face variant from
