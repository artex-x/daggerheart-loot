R4 purchase requests - mocks

Task persist-4-requests. Each file shows every state at 960 px and at 360 px side by side, with the app's tokens and component rules inline. B4.1 is the database half and draws nothing; every screen belongs to B4.2. Open the files from disk; the thumbnails load from the repository's img/thumb/.

  • send.html - A signed-out reader ticks entries on a share link and sends them to the owner: the button, sending, sent, and the four refusals. (section 6.3 "Send"; B4.2)
  • status.html - What the requester sees after a send: the toast and the unchanged page, the lowered stock after the owner applies, and the rejected one-line option (owner's feedback 2026-09-27). (section 6.3 "After the send", section 13 item 3; B4.2)
  • flow-b.html - A signed-in reader adds the selection to their own list and is asked to notify the owner; the remembered "always" answer. (section 6.3 "Flow b"; B4.2)
  • owner-panel.html - The owner's Requests panel: pending, the over-stock refusal and «Принять доступное», decisions of this page load, empty, and a refused decision. (section 6.3 "Owner panel"; B4.2)
  • index-card.html - The pending-request line on a list card of `#/lists`. (section 6.3 "Index"; B4.2)
  • display-row.html - The Display section's notify row: today, the owner's choice (a select with the new wording and the hint), and the rejected segmented control that overflows at 360 px. (section 6.3 "Display row", section 13 question 2, section 11 "Display row edits"; B4.2)
