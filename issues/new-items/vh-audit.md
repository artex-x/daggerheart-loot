# Card text audit - Escape the Veiled Halls

Question: will the text be visible on cards, or must it be cleaned up?

Answer: no cleanup is needed. All 15 loot entries are one to three sentences. The longest is Demonic Book (184 characters), the shortest 44; the catalog's median description is 125 characters, its 90th percentile 278 and its longest 874 (measured 2026-11-05). Every text is verbatim from the loot tables and fits a card without a rules card.

What is not loot and is left out: the 4 escape rooms (environments), the adversaries (Risen Witch, Risen Guardian, The Risen Dead, Demon of Vanity, Cursed Mannequin, Defective Clone, Mage Clone, Saboteur, Arch-Mage Aliessa Kanlor), the room hazards and the GM notes. The book has no weapons or armor, only "Tier 2 equipment" that the GM may place in the corpse heap and the wardrobes, with no names.

## Book notes

- Demonic Book: the PDF text layer drops the hyphens ("On a 16", "On a 79"); the printed page reads 1-6, 7-9 and 10-12, which the draft uses.
- Radioactive Syringe: "radioactive" is the book's word; kept.
- The book prints each room's loot as its own table (d4, d4, d3, d4), so a record has a `room` and a `roll` inside its room, as Many Worlds Tavern records have a realm.
