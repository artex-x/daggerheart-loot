# Card text audit - The Last Round Table

Question: the appendix has a lot of text. Will it be visible on cards, or must it be cleaned up?

Catalog reference (1459 records, measured 2026-11-05): median description 125 characters, 90th percentile 278, 99th percentile 574, longest 874 (Vault of Ages, Twilight Deck). The print card shrinks the rules text to fit, down to a floor of 2.2 cqw (about 4 pt on a 63 mm card, `FEATURES.md`, print fitting); the longest shipped record (874) is the practical ceiling, and a record near it prints small. On screen the record page has no limit. Rules that need a table or a procedure belong in a collapsed rules card (`refs`), which the record page and the copied text carry, as for Pistolheart.

Rule used for the proposed text: keep the first descriptive sentence(s) and every rule; drop flavor, GM roleplay notes and footnote jokes (the catalog ships no flavor text: decisions of 2026-10-18 and Arazo's GM notes). Weapons keep features only, as every catalog weapon does. Sentences are verbatim; 5 entries have a book typo noted below.

## Book typos (kept or fixed in the draft)

- Alewife Almanac: "Almenac" is printed in the feature; the cover says Almanac.
- Blessed Horseshoe: "social roles" is a book typo for "social rolls": keep as printed in the English and report it.
- Deed to the Last Round Table: "it's" in the Clean Up Song (rules card) is a book typo for "its".
- Apocalypse Ale: The book prints "unsettingly" (for "unsettlingly"): kept as printed in the draft; the owner decides.
- Gravedraught Ale: "an Difficulty 12" is a book typo for "a Difficulty 12".
